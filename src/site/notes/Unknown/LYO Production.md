#Definitions #unsure-or-needs-to-be-finished

LYO Production (room 1265, ISO 07) is a room on the first floor of the [[Buildings/Feynman\|Feynman]] building where lyophilization takes place. It is 2,918 SF and sits adjacent to the LYO Equipment room and the LYO Finishing rooms. Vials processed here move downstream to [[Unknown/LYO Finishing A\|LYO Finishing A]] and [[Definitions/LYO Finishing B\|LYO Finishing B]] for labeling and packaging.
