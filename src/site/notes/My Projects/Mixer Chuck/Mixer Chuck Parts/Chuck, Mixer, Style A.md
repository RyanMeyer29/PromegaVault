#Definitions

The Style A chuck is a 316L stainless steel collet-style mixer chuck designed by [[People/Travis Beyer\|Travis Beyer]]. It is part of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] redesign project. The chuck threads onto the bottom of a mixer shaft and uses a collet system to grip the impeller shaft -- the same approach CNC machines use to hold tooling.

From bottom to top: the threaded base accepts the collet nut (SAP 1016457). A tapered hole seats the collet (SAP 1016456), which contracts as the nut is tightened. Above that is a small shoulder with a ~1/4" diameter hole approximately 1/4" deep -- this accepts the head of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck Parts/Chuck Tool, Mixer\|Chuck Tool, Mixer]] during installation and removal. A larger shoulder above that acts as a mechanical stop against the mixer geometry. The main shaft steps back down in diameter and has two snap ring grooves surrounding the bearing surface to keep the chuck locked in place.

Style A has a longer upper portion that steps down to a snap ring groove, then steps down again to a smaller cylindrical section with a flat for a set screw. The exact drive connection type is unconfirmed -- no in-service mixers currently use this style. It is assumed to use a Lovejoy connector, likely a smaller one than Styles B and C, but this has not been verified by disassembly.

There are no active equipment numbers assigned to Style A. [[People/Jacob Holze\|Jake]] has requested at least one be ordered as a standby in case units in storage require it.

SAP#: 1016437
Material: 316L SS
Finish: 24 RA
