#Definitions

The Style B chuck is a 316L stainless steel collet-style mixer chuck designed by [[People/Travis Beyer\|Travis Beyer]]. It is part of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] redesign project. The chuck threads onto the bottom of a mixer shaft and uses a collet system to grip the impeller shaft.

From bottom to top: the threaded base accepts the collet nut (SAP 1016457). A tapered hole seats the collet (SAP 1016456), which contracts as the nut is tightened. Above that is a small shoulder with a ~1/4" diameter hole approximately 1/4" deep -- this accepts the head of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck Parts/Chuck Tool, Mixer\|Chuck Tool, Mixer]] during installation and removal. A larger shoulder above that acts as a mechanical stop against the mixer geometry. The main shaft steps back down in diameter and has two snap ring grooves surrounding the bearing surface to keep the chuck locked in place.

Style B continues above the bearing area for a short distance before stepping down to a slightly smaller diameter. This section has two large flat surfaces that accept set screws for a Lovejoy spider coupling connector. The end face is functionally flat aside from the small center hole left by the lathe dead center. Used on all BTC X5P33 mixers.

SAP#: 1016438
Material: 316L SS
Finish: 24 RA
Drawing#: 1016438

Equipment numbers: 102956, 112142, 122278, 122277, 102943 (all BTC, Mixer X5P33)
