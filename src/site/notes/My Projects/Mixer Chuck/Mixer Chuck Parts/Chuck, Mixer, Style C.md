#Definitions

The Style C chuck is a 316L stainless steel collet-style mixer chuck designed by [[People/Travis Beyer\|Travis Beyer]]. It is part of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] redesign project. The chuck threads onto the bottom of a mixer shaft and uses a collet system to grip the impeller shaft.

From bottom to top: the threaded base accepts the collet nut (SAP 1016457). A tapered hole seats the collet (SAP 1016456), which contracts as the nut is tightened. Above that is a small shoulder with a ~1/4" diameter hole approximately 1/4" deep -- this accepts the head of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck Parts/Chuck Tool, Mixer\|Chuck Tool, Mixer]] during installation and removal. A larger shoulder above that acts as a mechanical stop against the mixer geometry. The main shaft steps back down in diameter and has two snap ring grooves surrounding the bearing surface to keep the chuck locked in place.

Style C is similar to Style B but the flat section for the Lovejoy connector is much smaller -- just one small flat to accept a single set screw. Style C also has a hole through the top that lines up with the shaft connection running through the Lovejoy coupling. Used on all IVD XSS1P33 mixers and assumed for the XSS5P33. Also used on the RDC XSS1P33 mixer (assumed, same model as IVD units).

SAP#: 1016439
Material: 316L SS
Finish: 24 RA
Drawing#: 1016439

Equipment numbers: 113417, 113418, 117266, 127950, 120019 (IVD), 102883 (RDC)
