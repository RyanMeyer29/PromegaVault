#Definitions

The Chuck Tool is a two-part assembly used to install and remove the mixer chuck from the mixer shaft. It is part of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] redesign project. The tool works like a screwdriver -- the cylindrical head inserts into the small ~1/4" diameter hole at the bottom of the chuck body, and the handle provides grip and torque. Used in combination with the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck Parts/Tool, Spanner, Mixer Chuck\|Tool, Spanner, Mixer Chuck]], the two tools apply opposing torque to the chuck body and collet cap simultaneously, making tightening and loosening much easier -- similar to using two wrenches on opposite ends of a nut and bolt.

The assembly consists of a Delrin handle (SAP 1016459A) and a 316L stainless steel tool end (SAP 1016459B). The Delrin handle protects surrounding surfaces during use. The steel tool end is a plain cylinder -- no special tip geometry, it just needs to fit the hole in the chuck body.

Assembly SAP#: 1016459
Material: 316L SS / DELRIN
Finish: AS MACHINED
Drawing#: 1016459

## Sub-Parts

### Chuck Tool Handle (SAP 1016459A)

The handle is machined from Delrin. It is cylindrical with a knurled grip surface and a 1/4-20 UNC threaded hole at each end to accept the tool end. The soft material protects surrounding equipment surfaces during use.

SAP#: 1016459A
Material: DELRIN
Finish: AS MACHINED
Drawing#: 1016459

### Chuck Tool Head (SAP 1016459B)

The tool end is a slender 316L stainless steel cylinder that inserts into the ~1/4" hole at the bottom of the chuck body. No special tip geometry -- the fit between the cylinder and the hole is what transmits torque. Threaded end connects to the Delrin handle.

SAP#: 1016459B
Material: 316L SS
Finish: AS MACHINED
Drawing#: 1016459
