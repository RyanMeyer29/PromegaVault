#Definitions

The Split Baffle Assembly is a 2L dip tube assembly used by the [[Definitions/In Vitro Dispensing\|IVD]] team. It is part of the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Assembly\|Dip Tube Baffle Assembly]] project done for [[People/Philip McKeefry\|Philip]]. The assembly consists of a central [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Pickup Rod, 2L\|Pickup Rod, 2L]] with a [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Two Barb Pickup, 2L\|Two Barb Pickup, 2L]] welded to the bottom and [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Split Baffle, 2L\|Split Baffle, 2L]] halves mounted on either side of the rod. The Delrin component referenced in the material callout is part of the baffle geometry.

The assembly is used as a sinker shroud in a 2L vessel. The barbed connectors on the pickup piece connect to two different sized tubes, suspected to be an input and output for fluid, though this has not been confirmed. The original assembly used an off-center barb piece that was redesigned and centered as part of this project.
