#Definitions

The 2L Split Baffle is a 316L stainless steel baffle component used in the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Split Baffle Assembly, 2L\|Split Baffle Assembly, 2L]]. It is part of the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Assembly\|Dip Tube Baffle Assembly]] project done for [[People/Philip McKeefry\|Philip]] in collaboration with the [[Definitions/In Vitro Dispensing\|IVD]] team. The baffle consists of two flat paddle-like halves that sit on either side of the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Pickup Rod, 2L\|Pickup Rod, 2L]], similar in profile to a cricket bat -- flat and wide with a cylindrical rod passing through the center. The split design allows the baffle to be assembled around the rod rather than threaded onto it.

The drawing for this part is dimensioned in millimeters, unlike the other parts in the assembly which use inches.
