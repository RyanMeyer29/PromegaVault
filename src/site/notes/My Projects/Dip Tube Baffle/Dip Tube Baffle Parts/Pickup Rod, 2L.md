#Definitions

The Pickup Rod is a 316L stainless steel rod that forms the central structural element of the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Split Baffle Assembly, 2L\|Split Baffle Assembly, 2L]]. It is part of the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Assembly\|Dip Tube Baffle Assembly]] project done for [[People/Philip McKeefry\|Philip]] in collaboration with the [[Definitions/In Vitro Dispensing\|IVD]] team. The rod runs vertically through the center of the split baffle and serves as the main shaft of the dip tube assembly. The [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Two Barb Pickup, 2L\|Two Barb Pickup, 2L]] is welded to the bottom of the rod. The [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Split Baffle, 2L\|Split Baffle, 2L]] halves sit on either side of the rod.
