#Definitions

The Two Barb Pickup is a small 316L stainless steel piece that is welded to the bottom of the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Pickup Rod, 2L\|Pickup Rod, 2L]] in the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Parts/Split Baffle Assembly, 2L\|Split Baffle Assembly, 2L]]. It is part of the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Assembly\|Dip Tube Baffle Assembly]] project done for [[People/Philip McKeefry\|Philip]] in collaboration with the [[Definitions/In Vitro Dispensing\|IVD]] team. The piece has two barbed hose connectors of different sizes to accommodate two different tube diameters -- one is suspected to be an input and one an output, though the exact function of each has not been confirmed.

On the opposite end of the barb connectors is a feature of unusual geometry suspected to either trap bubbles or redirect them away from the tube inlets, preventing them from entering the fluid path. The exact function of this bubble feature is unconfirmed.

The original part was off-center and this redesign corrected that, mirroring and centering the barb geometry. The piece was redesigned to accommodate two specific tube sizes used by the IVD team.
