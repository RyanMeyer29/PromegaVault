## Key Confirmations

The 5606 is the probe that dangles, per [[People/David Velazquez\|David]] (Teams, 2026-04-02). This was the design driver for the lower tube carrier concept.

Only the 5622-32 goes to 280°C, per [[People/David Velazquez\|David]] (Teams, 2026-04-01). The other probes do not need to survive the 280°C bath.

The 5622-05 and 5622-10 are grouped in the [[My Projects/Calibration Probe Fixture/Technical Specs/Bath and Probe Spec\|Bath and Probe Spec]] and treated as functionally equivalent for fixture purposes. [[People/David Velazquez\|David]] confirmed designing around the 5622-10 dimensions will also accommodate the 0.5 mm 5622-05.

The 5628-12-S is in scope per the [Bath and Probe Spec]]. SAP number 135173 confirmed; probe received and measured in person on 2026-05-28, dimensions match the datasheet. The product manual is in Technical Specs: [[5626_5628_Probe.pdf](/img/user/My%20Projects/Calibration%20Probe%20Fixture/Technical%20Specs/Bath%20and%20Probe%20Spec.md).
