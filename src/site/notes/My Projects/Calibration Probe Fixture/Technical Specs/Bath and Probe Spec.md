#Definitions

The Bath and Probe Spec is the live Excel sheet maintained by the [[Definitions/Metrology\|Metrology]] team that defines the in-scope baths and probes for the [[My Projects/Calibration Probe Fixture/Calibration Probe Fixture\|Calibration Probe Fixture]] project. It is the authoritative source for what hardware the fixture must accommodate. Originally shared by [[People/David Velazquez\|David]] on 2026-03-31 via SharePoint.

Open the live file: [Bath and Probe Spec](https://promega.sharepoint.com/sites/Metrology-Team/Shared%20Documents/General/Team%20Projects/2025%20Fluke%20Acquisition%20Project/Bath%20and%20Probe%20Spec.xlsx)

## Contents (as of 2026-04-01)

The sheet lists four baths (7380, 7381-26, 7341-16, 6331-156) with their temperature ranges, media, well opening sizes, and depths. It also lists five probe entries (5622-05/-10 grouped — [5622_Probe_Technical_Data.pdf](/img/user/My%20Projects/Calibration%20Probe%20Fixture/Technical%20Specs/5622_Probe_Technical_Data.pdf), 5622-32 — [5622_Probe_Technical_Data.pdf](/img/user/My%20Projects/Calibration%20Probe%20Fixture/Technical%20Specs/5622_Probe_Technical_Data.pdf), 5606 — [5606_5607_Probe.pdf](/img/user/My%20Projects/Calibration%20Probe%20Fixture/Technical%20Specs/5606_5607_Probe.pdf), 5628-12-S — [5626_5628_Probe.pdf](/img/user/My%20Projects/Calibration%20Probe%20Fixture/Technical%20Specs/5626_5628_Probe.pdf), 5623B — [5623B_Probe.pdf](/img/user/My%20Projects/Calibration%20Probe%20Fixture/Technical%20Specs/5623B_Probe.pdf)) with diameters, lengths, ideal submersion depths, and ideal number of holes per fixture.

A pulled snapshot from 2026-05-15 surfaced new information not previously documented in the vault: the 5628-12-S probe and the 7380 bath with its different opening size (3 x 4 in vs 4.7 x 6.8 in for the others). These should be cross-referenced against the live sheet before any design freeze.
