#Definitions

The Fluke In-House SOP is the live Word document maintained by the [[Definitions/Metrology\|Metrology]] team that defines the standard operating procedure for in-house calibration and verification of Fluke temperature probes. Originally shared by [[People/David Velazquez\|David]] on 2026-04-01 as "Fluke In-House 3.0" (the project notes refer to it as "Fluke Draft, draft rev 52").

The document covers the Fluke 1523 reference thermometer (the instrument being calibrated), the 1586A Super-DAQ module (used with the SPRT during configuration), and TPW module 9210 (the Triple Point of Water bath unit). The procedure addresses the probe models 5606, 5622-32, 5622-05, 5622-10, and 5623B; the 5628-12-S may or may not be covered (verify in the live document).

Software referenced in the SOP: TableWare for generating ITS-90 coefficients and IO ToolKit for uploading coefficients to the 1523 via serial connection.

When the latest version is needed, the file should be pulled live from SharePoint rather than relying on this stub note's snapshot.
