#My-Projects

A 1" x 11" HDPE blocker piece for [[People/Paul Doll\|Paul]]'s flipper machine. The flipper machine is a tray that rotates and flips vials or bottles. Paul believes this is needed because the upstream packaging brings bottles in upside down and they need to be reoriented for the rest of the line.

The original blocker piece broke. Paul came into [[Buildings/Lamarr\|Lamarr]] one day with the metal tray portion of the machine and needed a quick fix. After he broke that one too, he handed off the redesign and fabrication to me since he was busy.

Status and action items at the top.

---

## Status

Complete. New blocker fabricated and returned to Paul, installed.

---

## Fabrication Workflow

1. Cut 1" x 11" rectangle of HDPE on the [[Fabrication/Zund\|Zund]]
2. Round corners on the belt sander
3. Drill and tap holes on either end for M4 screws

The blocker mounts to one end of the metal tray using M4 screws.
