#Definitions

The Transformer Plan is a Promega organizational development program for structured personal and professional growth. It was formerly called the "90-day plan" — that language was retired org-wide by [[People/Chuck York\|Chuck York]] on 2026-06-06. The updated framework uses three time horizons: Near (weeks), Arc (months), and Horizon (up to one year). Goals remain SMART but no longer carry a fixed end date.

Each cycle ends with a reflection prompt called the Philosopher's Stone. [[People/Mindy Becker\|Mindy Becker]] sent the Cycle 1 Philosopher's Stone prompt on 2026-06-12.
