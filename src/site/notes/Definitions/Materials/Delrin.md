#Definitions

Delrin (also known as acetal or POM — polyoxymethylene) is a semi-crystalline thermoplastic known for its high stiffness, low friction, and good machinability. It is a common engineering plastic for precision machined parts. At Promega it is stocked at [[Buildings/Lamarr\|Lamarr]] and used in fabricated assemblies. It has a maximum continuous service temperature of roughly 90–120°C, which makes it unsuitable for high-temperature applications.

Encountered as the handle material in the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck Parts/Chuck Tool, Mixer\|Chuck Tool, Mixer]] (SAP 1016459, described as "Stainless/Delrin"), and evaluated and ruled out as a bushing material for the [[My Projects/Calibration Probe Fixture/Calibration Probe Fixture\|Calibration Probe Fixture]] due to its low temperature ceiling.
