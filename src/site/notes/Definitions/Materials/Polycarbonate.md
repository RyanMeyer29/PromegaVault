#Definitions

Polycarbonate (PC) is a tough, transparent thermoplastic with good impact resistance and moderate chemical resistance. It is softer than acrylic (Rockwell M~70 vs acrylic ~M85–M105), has a slight blue/yellow edge tint on cut surfaces, and is significantly more forgiving to bend and form than acrylic at equivalent thicknesses. It is available in sheet stock at [[Buildings/Lamarr\|Lamarr]].

At Promega, polycarbonate is the material used for the [[My Projects/Gel Electrophoresis Tray\|Gel Electrophoresis Tray]] and is the suspected original material of the [[My Projects/Lyo Clear Cover\|Lyo Clear Cover]] (initially assumed to be acrylic; physical evidence including edge tint, scratch behavior, and flex characteristics all point to PC — pending confirmation via acetone test).
