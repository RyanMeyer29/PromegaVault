#Definitions

PEEK (Polyether Ether Ketone) is a high-performance semi-crystalline thermoplastic with excellent mechanical properties, chemical resistance, and a continuous service temperature of approximately 250°C. It is machinable to tight tolerances on a lathe or mill, making it well-suited for precision components. It is available as stock rod from suppliers like McMaster-Carr.

At Promega, PEEK is the leading candidate bushing material for the [[My Projects/Calibration Probe Fixture/Calibration Probe Fixture\|Calibration Probe Fixture]] drop-in bushing system. The frame is confirmed 316L stainless; the bushing material is still under evaluation between PEEK and [[Fabrication/Formlabs Form 3L\|Formlabs High Temp Resin]]. PEEK's 250°C continuous rating covers all baths except the 280°C high-temp bath (6331), where it may be marginal. David Velazquez confirmed vent holes are acceptable on the lid, which may keep vapor temperatures below the bath setpoint near the bushing — worth verifying before ruling PEEK out at the high-temp bath.
