#Definitions

316L stainless steel is an austenitic stainless steel alloy and a common engineering material at Promega. The "L" designation indicates low carbon content, which improves weldability and corrosion resistance compared to standard 316. It offers excellent resistance to corrosion in wet, acidic, and chloride-containing environments. At Promega it appears in fabricated parts requiring chemical resistance and dimensional stability across wide temperature ranges.

Encountered as the confirmed frame material for the [[My Projects/Calibration Probe Fixture/Calibration Probe Fixture\|Calibration Probe Fixture]] (temperature range -80°C to 280°C, silicone oil and ethanol bath environment), and as the base material for the [[My Projects/Dip Tube Baffle/Dip Tube Baffle Assembly\|Dip Tube Baffle Assembly]] (2L sinker shroud, SAP 1015703).
