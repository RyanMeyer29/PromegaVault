#Definitions

A design review is a formal engineering checkpoint where a design is evaluated by stakeholders before moving to the next phase of development or production. At Promega, design reviews are part of the formal Design and Development Process, governed by QSOP14.003 (regulated products) and QSOP14.004 (non-regulated products). See [[Definitions/Quality/Design Control Process\|Design Control Process]] for the Promega-specific framework.
