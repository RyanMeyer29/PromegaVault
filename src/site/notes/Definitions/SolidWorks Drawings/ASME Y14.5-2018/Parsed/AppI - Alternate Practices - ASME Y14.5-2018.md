## Overview

Appendix I is a mandatory appendix describing alternative practices that are still compliant with Y14.5-2018. These are practices carried forward from older standards that remain acceptable.

## I-1 Plus and Minus Tolerancing for Location

Direct plus/minus tolerancing (non-GD&T) may be used to locate features. When plus/minus tolerances are used to locate features of size, the tolerance zone is a square or rectangular zone, not a cylindrical zone.

This means a hole located by ±.005 in both X and Y has a square tolerance zone with .010 sides — the actual cylindrical tolerance zone at the corners of the square is larger than the specified ±.005 by a factor of 1.414 (√2). This is less favorable than a circular zone from true position.

Important: This practice applies tolerances from the title block general tolerance, which requires careful interpretation. For precise location control, true position with basic dimensions is preferred.

## I-2 Interpretation of Limits

The use of plus/minus location tolerances introduces tolerance accumulation along dimension chains. This is in contrast to datum-referenced position tolerances which eliminate accumulation.

Note: Per the foreword of Y14.5-2018, plus/minus tolerancing for location is likely to be REMOVED in the next revision of the standard. GD&T with true position and basic dimensions is the preferred and future-proof practice.
