ASME Y14.5-2018

Section 1
Scope
1.1 INTRODUCTION
This Standard establishes uniform practices for stating
and interpreting GD&T and related requirements for use
on engineering drawings and in related documents. For
procedural requirements governing the preparation of
engineering drawings and related documents, see ASME
Y14.100.
Practices unique to architectural and civil engineering
and welding symbology are not included.
1.2 GENERAL
(a) All drawing types and methods of product definition
that cite ASME Y14.5 as the drawing standard shall follow
the practices established within this Standard.
(b) Each feature on a drawing shall be fully defined and
toleranced. No drawing requirement shall be left to
assumption.
(c) Dimensions and tolerances apply only at the drawing
level where they are specified. A dimension or tolerance
specified for a given product definition level (e.g.,
detailed part drawing) is not mandatory for that product
at any other level (e.g., an assembly drawing).
1.3 REFERENCE TO THIS STANDARD
(a) On drawings where all dimensioning and tolerancing
is in accordance with this Standard, the reference shall be
shown in or near the title block as follows: DIMENSIONING
AND TOLERANCING PER ASME Y14.5-2018.
(b) On drawings where some of the dimensioning and
tolerancing is in accordance with this Standard, and some
is in accordance with another standard, a note shall be
used to identify which dimensions and tolerances are in
accordance with each standard.
1.4 ASME Y14 SERIES CONVENTIONS
(a) Mandatory requirements are indicated by the word
"shall."
(b) Guidance and/or recommended practices are indicated by the words "should" or "may."
(c) When a referenced standard is cited in the text, the
cited version shall take precedence.
1.5 DRAWINGS WITHOUT REFERENCE TO A STANDARD
When a drawing does not reference a standard for
dimensioning and tolerancing and the drawing is in U.S.
customary units, the practices established by this
Standard should apply. When the drawing uses metric
units, the ISO standards should apply.
1.6 REFERENCE TO GAGING
(a) Reference to gaging standards in this Standard is
not an endorsement of any specific gaging practice, as
gaging methods may vary.
(b) Functional gages illustrate possible gaging methods and may not represent the only or preferred gaging
method.
1.7 SYMBOLS
Adoption of symbols indicating dimensional requirements, as shown in Nonmandatory Appendix C, does
not preclude the use of equivalent terms or abbreviations
when the use of symbols is considered impractical.
