#Definitions

The Parlec ER32 collet is the stock collet used in the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] redesign. It is an unmodified purchased part sourced from MSC Industrial. No drawing is required for this part — it ships as-is from the manufacturer with no Promega modifications.

The collet is coolant-sealed and sized for a 3/4" shaft. It seats inside the collet cap and is clamped by the stop via an M4 x 0.7 screw threaded into the collet cap.

Manufacturer: Parlec
Manufacturer part number: EROSC32-3/4
MSC part number: 36590644
MSC product page: https://www.mscdirect.com/product/details/36590644
Collet series: ER32
Collet size: 3/4"
Through coolant: Yes (sealed)
Material: Manufacturer specification (440C stainless typical for ER collets — verify if needed)
