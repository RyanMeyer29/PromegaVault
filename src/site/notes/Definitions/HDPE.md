#Definitions

HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) is a thermoplastic polymer commonly used as a fabrication material at Promega for machined and cut parts. It is available in sheet stock at [[Buildings/Lamarr\|Lamarr]] and is cut on the [[Fabrication/Zund\|Zund]]. It is chosen for its machinability, chemical resistance, low friction, and toughness. Encountered in the [[My Projects/Flipper Tray Blocker\|Flipper Tray Blocker]] and [[My Projects/96 Well Bracket\|96 Well Bracket]] projects.
