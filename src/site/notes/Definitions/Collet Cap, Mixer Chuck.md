#Definitions

The Collet Cap is a 440C stainless steel nut that threads onto the bottom of the mixer chuck body. It is part of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] redesign project. When tightened, it forces the collet (SAP 1016456) up into the tapered seat inside the chuck, causing the collet to contract and grip the impeller shaft. Loosening the cap releases the collet and allows the impeller shaft to be removed.

The cap has a modified slot geometry on its face that mates with the [[Definitions/Tool, Spanner, Mixer Chuck\|Tool, Spanner, Mixer Chuck]] for tightening and loosening. It also has two tapped M4 x 0.7 holes for mounting the [[Definitions/Stop, Mixer Chuck\|Stop, Mixer Chuck]], which prevents the cap from backing off too far during use. It is a modified stock component -- base part is Maxxtooling MX-CCRPR032. Used across all three chuck styles.

SAP#: 1016457
Material: 440C SS
Finish: AS MACHINED
Drawing#: 1016457
