#Definitions

The Stop is a small 316L stainless steel part that attaches to the collet nut on the mixer chuck assembly. It is part of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] redesign project. Its purpose is to prevent the nut from being over-tightened or over-loosened during installation and removal. Over-loosening is the primary concern -- if the nut backs off too far it could fall off the chuck entirely and drop into the product being mixed.

The stop uses a clearance hole for an M4 x 0.7 screw to mount to the nut. It is used across all three chuck styles (Style A, B, and C) and is engraved with the drawing number for identification.

SAP#: 1016455
Material: 316L SS
Finish: AS MACHINED
Drawing#: 1016455
