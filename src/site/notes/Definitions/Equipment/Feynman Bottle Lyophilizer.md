#Definitions

The Feynman Bottle Lyophilizer is a [[Definitions/Lyophilizer\|Lyophilizer]] used during the sealing process for 1 mL, 10 mL, and 100 mL bottles in the [[Definitions/LYO Finishing B\|finishing area]] within [[Buildings/Feynman\|Feynman]]. It feeds into the downstream finishing rooms where vials are labeled and packaged. The [[My Projects/Lyo Clear Cover\|Lyo Clear Cover]] and [[My Projects/Falling Vial Bin\|Falling Vial Bin]] projects both relate to equipment in this area.
