#Definitions

The Spanner Tool is a custom 316L stainless steel wrench designed to mate with the unusual slot geometry of the [[Definitions/Collet Cap, Mixer Chuck\|Collet Cap, Mixer Chuck]]. It is part of the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] redesign project. Used in combination with the [[Definitions/Chuck Tool, Mixer\|Chuck Tool, Mixer]] to tighten or loosen the collet cap -- the two tools work together like two wrenches on a nut and bolt, applying opposing torque to the chuck body and cap simultaneously for easier installation and removal.

The assembly consists of two sub-parts welded together: the head (SAP 1016458A) and the handle (SAP 1016458B). Both are 316L stainless steel. The head has tab geometry that engages the slots on the collet cap face. The handle is a 5" cylindrical rod that provides the lever arm for torque. The connection between head and handle is welded.

Assembly SAP#: 1016458
Material: 316L SS
Finish: AS MACHINED
Drawing#: 1016458

## Sub-Parts

### Spanner Tool Head (SAP 1016458A)

The spanner head is the business end of the tool. It has five equally spaced tabs at 60 degree spacing that engage the matching slot geometry on the collet cap face. The tabs have a specific profile to fit the cap slots without slipping.

SAP#: 1016458A
Material: 316L SS
Finish: AS MACHINED
Drawing#: 1016458

### Spanner Tool Handle (SAP 1016458B)

A straight 316L stainless steel cylindrical rod, 5" long, that provides the lever arm for applying torque to the spanner head. Welded to the head at assembly.

SAP#: 1016458B
Material: 316L SS
Finish: AS MACHINED
Drawing#: 1016458
