#Definitions

The Design and Development Process at Promega is the formal framework governing how new products and processes are designed, reviewed, and released. It is split into two procedures depending on product type: QSOP14.003 covers regulated products (current Rev 3, effective 2024-03-06) and QSOP14.004 covers non-regulated products (current Rev 1, effective 2024-03-06). Both live under the Quality System and are administered through [[Definitions/QA and Regulatory Affairs\|QA and Regulatory Affairs]]. Design reviews are formal checkpoints within this process where stakeholders evaluate a design before it advances to the next phase.
