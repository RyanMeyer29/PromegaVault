#Definitions

Change Control (CC) is a formal process at Promega for identifying, evaluating, approving, and implementing changes. It covers a wide scope including changes to facilities, utilities, equipment, products, processes, software, and documents. It is governed by QP24 (Rev 1) and procedurally by QSOP24.009 (Rev 9, effective Jul 20, 2026). The system used to open and manage CCs at Promega is ETQ Reliance. Change Control is a key step in deploying new or modified production parts and equipment, and is something I encounter regularly in fill/finish and GMP equipment engineering work.

A CC moves through four lifecycle stages: Initiation & Planning, Evaluations, Implement Changes, and Final Review & Close. During Initiation & Planning the author describes the change and identifies all required action documents. During Evaluations, assigned evaluators review the CC and the author provides justification. During Implement Changes, the documented actions are executed and signed off. Final Review & Close completes the record.

A CC has seven sections. The first four are text fields typed directly into ETQ Reliance. The last three are Word documents attached to the CC.

Text fields: [[Definitions/Quality/CC Sections/Brief Description\|Brief Description]] — [[Definitions/Quality/CC Sections/Additional Description\|Additional Description]] — [[Definitions/Quality/CC Sections/Reason for Change\|Reason for Change]] — [[Definitions/Quality/CC Sections/Justification for Change\|Justification for Change]]

Action documents: [[Definitions/Quality/CC Sections/Implementation Actions\|Implementation Actions]] — [[Definitions/Quality/CC Sections/Testing Actions\|Testing Actions]] — [[Definitions/Quality/CC Sections/Impacted Documentation\|Impacted Documentation]]

The Change Champion is the person responsible for driving the CC to completion. That is typically the engineer who initiated the change. The Change Initiator and Champion can be the same person or different people. The Module Coordinator (QA Systems) can assist through the process and handles voiding records when needed.

Eight [[Definitions/Quality/Change Types\|CC types]] are defined in QSOP24.009 — selecting the correct type determines which phases and evaluations ETQ will prompt for. For large projects with multiple interdependent changes, a [[Definitions/Quality/Program-Level Change Control\|Program-Level Change Control]] is opened first as a parent record before initiating the individual CCs underneath it.

Minor documentation changes with no or minimal impact on product or process are handled through a Change Order (CO) in ETQ's Document Control module rather than a full CC. See QSOP23.001 for the CO process.
