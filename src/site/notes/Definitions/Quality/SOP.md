#Definitions

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP). Confirmed via SharePoint. Formal, versioned documents outlining specific tasks or processes at Promega. Follow a numbered format (e.g. SOP11A004). SOPs are managed through [[Software/EtQ Reliance\|EtQ Reliance]] and are part of the Promega Quality Management System.
