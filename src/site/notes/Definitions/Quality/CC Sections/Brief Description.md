#Definitions

The Brief Description is the one-line summary of what the change is. It appears first in the Initiation & Planning stage and populates the header of all three action documents verbatim, so it has to be short enough to render cleanly there.

Hard limit in ETQ is 200 characters. Working target is 100 characters or fewer. Plain text only — no formatting. It should answer what is changing and where or on what system. Think of it as a subject line: complete but minimal. Do not editorialize.

To write it you need: what is being changed, and where or on what equipment, document, or system.

> [!info]- Examples
>
> Simple single-scope change:
> Add caustic wash step to Media Prep CIP.
>
> Equipment repair:
> Repair outlet valve support structure on TK-1010 in Feynman ECR suite 2228.
>
> Multi-scope change (name both concisely):
> Add CIP Recipe Status Verification to Biokill Sanitization Initial Conditions and PURW Request Logic Update at the Arnold Center
>
