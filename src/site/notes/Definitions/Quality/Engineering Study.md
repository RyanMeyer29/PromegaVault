#Definitions

An Engineering Study is a structured internal process at Promega for evaluating and designing solutions to equipment or workflow problems. It is a formal but internally defined process used before a full [[Definitions/Quality/Change Control\|Change Control]] is opened. The output typically informs whether and how a change should be made.
