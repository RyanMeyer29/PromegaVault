#Definitions

Form, Fit or Function (FFF) is a framework used at Promega to assess whether a change meaningfully alters a part or product's physical characteristics, interface, or intended performance. It is the primary test for determining whether a change qualifies as a Product and Process Change type in the [[Definitions/Quality/Change Control\|CC]] process. If a change has a substantial or potentially substantial impact on any of the three, it likely requires a Product and Process Change rather than a simpler Equipment or Quality System Change.

Form — the shape, size, dimensions, mass, weight, and other parameters that uniquely characterize an item.

Fit — the ability of an item to physically interface or interconnect with, or become an integral part of, another item.

Function — the actions an item is designed to perform.

## Examples of Changes That May Impact Form, Fit or Function

Product design changes: final formulation or composition, product concentration, instrument or accessory design, labels or certificate of analysis content, primary containers or secondary packaging, compatibility with other materials or components.

Manufacturing changes: raw material composition or supplier, manufacturing equipment substitution, method of producing or processing, manufacturing scale, change in subcontractors, manufacturing site transfer.

Quality control changes: QC specifications, QC test methods, inspection procedures.

Shipping and storage changes: delivery method, storage temperature, shelf life when shortened, storage conditions.
