#Fabrication

McMaster-Carr is an industrial parts supplier used for sourcing hardware, raw stock, and specialty components. At Promega, it is a primary source for fasteners, rod stock (including [[Definitions/Materials/PEEK\|PEEK]], [[Definitions/Materials/316L Stainless Steel\|316L Stainless Steel]], and other materials), and standard hardware used in fabricated assemblies.
