#Fabrication

The Formlabs Form 3L is a large-format SLA (stereolithography) resin 3D printer available in the fabrication area. It produces high-detail parts using UV-cured liquid resin, and is suited for larger prints than the standard Form 3.

Formlabs High Temp Resin is available and has been used on this printer. It is being evaluated as a bushing material for the [[My Projects/Calibration Probe Fixture/Calibration Probe Fixture\|Calibration Probe Fixture]] project. Post-cure process: standard UV cure followed by a lab oven bake at 160°C for 180 minutes. Temperature resistance is approximately 238°C HDT at 0.45 MPa.
