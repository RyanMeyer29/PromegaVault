#Buildings

Lamarr is a Promega building that houses the machine shop, packaging area, and fabrication equipment. The machine shop is where machined parts are produced; shop requests go through [[People/Travis Beyer\|Travis Beyer]] and [[People/Kent Toepfer\|Kent Toepfer]]. Fabrication equipment includes the [[Fabrication/Zund\|Zund]] CNC cutting table, a [[Fabrication/Water Jet\|Water Jet]] (12" × 12" form factor), and a suite of [[Fabrication/3D Printers\|3D Printers]]: [[Fabrication/Markforged Industrial X7\|Markforged Industrial X7]], [[Fabrication/Markforged Desktop Onyx\|Markforged Desktop Onyx]], and [[Fabrication/Formlabs Form 3L\|Formlabs Form 3L]]. [[Definitions/HDPE\|HDPE]] sheet stock is available here for cutting on the Zund.
