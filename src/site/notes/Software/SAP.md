#Software

SAP is Promega's enterprise resource planning (ERP) system, used for part numbers, equipment numbers, and the parts management process. Every production part and piece of equipment has a unique SAP number. Getting a SAP number from the SAP Analyst is the first step in the [[Definitions/Parts Process/Parts Process\|Parts Process]] before a drawing is created or a shop request is submitted.

The primary SAP contact for part number requests is [[People/Gary McCauley\|Gary McCauley]]. [[People/Nikki Masterson\|Nikki Masterson]] is also a contact for this. For fewer than 5 parts, requests go through the [[Definitions/Parts Process/Parts Order Form\|Parts Order Form]] (Smartsheet). For 5 or more parts, the [[Definitions/Parts Process/Multiple Materials Request Spreadsheet\|Multiple Materials Request Spreadsheet]] is used instead.
