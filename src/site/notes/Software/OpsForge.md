#Software

OpsForge is a shared Claude project used by the [[Definitions/Operations Engineering\|Operations Engineering]] team at Promega. It serves as a centralized AI resource for the team to use on real operations work.

Elizabeth Duncan organizes "Grounds & Forge" sessions, coffee and donuts meetups where team members share how they have used OpsForge on actual problems.
