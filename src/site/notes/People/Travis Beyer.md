#People

Travis Beyer is a machinist at [[Buildings/Lamarr\|Lamarr]]. He designed the 3 collet-style chuck options (Style A, B, C) for the [[My Projects/Mixer Chuck/Mixer Chuck\|Mixer Chuck]] project and built the SolidWorks models. The styles correspond to the 3 different motor mount configurations the mixer manufacturer uses. Style B maps to the X5P33 (BTC), Style C maps to the XSS1P33 and XSS5P33 (IVD and RDC). No in-service mixers currently use Style A. Travis will receive the shop request once drawings are approved and spares quantities are confirmed.
