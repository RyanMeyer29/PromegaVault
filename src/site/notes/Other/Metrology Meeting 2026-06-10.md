## Bushing Material — PEEK vs High Temp Resin

The frame is planned as 316L stainless. The open question is what to make the drop-in bushings from.

| | PEEK | Formlabs High Temp Resin |
|---|---|---|
| Machinability | Excellent — lathe/mill | N/A — 3D printed |
| Tolerancing | Tight tolerances achievable | Less precise, layer lines |
| Temp rating | ~250°C continuous | ~238°C HDT at 0.45 MPa |
| Lifetime | Long, well-characterized | Less known in oil baths |
| Cost/effort | Stock rod + lathe time | Printer + post-cure oven |
